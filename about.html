The Intersection

People + Process + Technology

Many companies believe buying new HR software will automatically solve their administrative problems. In reality, software installed over broken processes only accelerates chaos.

NK Velora Ventures does not simply recommend tools. We first analyze and redesign the underlying business process, select or optimize the appropriate technology, and then construct an operating system that internal teams can reliably execute.
